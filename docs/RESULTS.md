# Recorded results

The following comparisons were recorded in the original 2020 academic report. They have not been repeated for this repository.

## Bench comparisons

| Quantity | Pair of readings compared | Approximate difference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Voltage | 246 V and 246.5 V | 0.2% |
| Current | 4.37 A and 4.40 A | 0.7% |
| Active power | 983 W and 1011 W | 3% |

Voltage was compared with a Fluke 117. Current and active power were compared with a Voltcraft meter. The report states that the latter had no accredited calibration.

## Interpretation

Each row describes an individual comparison point. The results show how closely the prototype and reference readings agreed under those conditions.

They do not establish an accuracy specification over the full measurement range. They also do not establish performance for other loads, temperatures or operating conditions.

## Main limitations

- Low-current readings were affected by changes in the sensor midpoint.
- Calibration was specific to the original measurement circuits.
- The quality of the reference instruments limited the comparisons.
- Accumulated energy totals were not validated by these comparisons.
